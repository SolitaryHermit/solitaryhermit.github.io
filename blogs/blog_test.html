Spectral Properties

(Spectral Gap Bound)

Let $\lambda_2(\mathbf{L})$ denote the algebraic connectivity (second smallest eigenvalue) of $\mathbf{L}$. If $G$ is connected, then:

$$ \lambda_2(\mathbf{L}) \ge \frac{1}{n \cdot \text{diam}(G)} $$
(Mixing Time Implication)

The random walk on graph $G$ converges to the uniform distribution in $O\left(\frac{\log n}{\lambda_2}\right)$ steps.
