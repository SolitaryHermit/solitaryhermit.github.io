About Me

Welcome to my homepage! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at Georgia State University under the supervision of Prof. Florian Enescu. My research focuses on Frobenius complexity and its applications in algebraic geometry, as well as some sign-pattern problems in combinatorial matrix theory. I am passionate about exploring the deep connections between algebra, combinatorics, and geometry, and I enjoy collaborating with fellow researchers in these areas.

In addition to my research, I have a strong interest in teaching and mentoring students. I have served as a teaching assistant or main instructor for various mathematics courses, ranging from introductory algebra to advanced calculus. I am committed to fostering an inclusive and engaging learning environment where students can develop their mathematical skills and confidence.

Finally, I am also working on a handbook for algebra researchers in a similar fashion to the Stacks Project (though with more detailed and accessible proofs!!). One can download a draft version of the book from here if interested (sorry for any existing typos...). As a companion to this project, I also plan to make some of the content publicly available online, so please check out this link if you are interested!
